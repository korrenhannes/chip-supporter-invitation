Help me understand day-to-day chip design and verification work

I'm Korren Hannes, a junior verification engineer doing this for personal learning. I'm hoping experienced engineers will share what their work is really like and help me understand the industry.

Who I hope to learn from

I'm looking for engineers with at least two years of hands-on RTL chip design or pre-silicon design-verification work who currently work at a fabless company, and people with at least two years currently developing or technically supporting EDA tools used directly in chip design or verification.

What the conversation is

It is a flexible conversation lasting 60 minutes about the daily and monthly work that takes the most time: tools, handoffs, bottlenecks, collaboration difficulties, and recurring integrations between chip designers and verification engineers. There is no compensation. Remote video is the default, voice-only is welcome, and meeting in person in Israel is possible. We can speak English or Hebrew.

Privacy

No recording, transcript, quotes, narrative notes, or identity-linked interview notes are retained. Contact and scheduling records are deleted 30 days after the interview or final decline. After an unanswered final follow-up, they are deleted after 90 days and only a keyed suppression status remains to prevent accidental re-contact.
