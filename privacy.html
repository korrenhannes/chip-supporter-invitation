Chip Intel Outreach privacy notice

Effective 16 September 2026. I’m Korren Hannes. Chip Intel Outreach helps me invite and schedule engineers for personal research conversations about day-to-day semiconductor work. It is not used to make employment decisions. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to korrenhannes@gmail.com.

Information I use

I may use provenance-recorded public professional information, relevant technical-work evidence, a contact route, our email conversation, and any availability or timezone you share. I use them to check whether an invitation is relevant, contact you about this research, respect a stop request, and arrange an interview if you agree. I do not use protected or sensitive traits to select participants.

Google access

With my authorization, the tool uses Gmail to send and reconcile project invitations and replies under a dedicated project label. It checks mailbox history identifiers and known project threads for replies; it does not fetch unrelated inbox message content. It may search for a reply from an exact active sender before a follow-up. Google Calendar access reads free/busy availability and creates or manages an event on my own calendar, with a Google Meet link, only after a participant selects a time. The event may include the participant as an attendee.

Processing and protection

Contact values and conversation content in my local project database are encrypted; keyed hashes support matching and suppression. Google OAuth grants are kept in macOS Keychain. Limited, sanitized conversation context may be processed by OpenAI Codex to draft an invitation or routine reply. Sensitive, unclear, legal, and privacy questions are escalated to me. I do not sell this information or share it for advertising.

Retention and your choices

I do not record interviews or retain transcripts, quotes, narrative notes, or identity-linked interview notes. Local conversation and scheduling content is purged 30 days after an interview or final decline, or 90 days after the third unanswered invitation. A minimal keyed suppression status and audit evidence may remain so I do not contact you again by mistake. This local purge does not delete copies already held in my Gmail or Google Calendar account; those remain subject to Google account controls. Reply to any invitation to stop contact, or email me to request access, correction, or deletion of local information, subject to the minimum needed to honor your request.

This website

This page has no form, analytics script, or tracking cookie. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process site access information under its own privacy practices. Updates to this notice will appear here.
